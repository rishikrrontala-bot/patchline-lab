Workflow

  1. 01 · Load recording

    Upload a CSV, confirm its columns and units, and select the sensors present. One channel is enough. Your files stay in this tab.

    CSV: comma delimiter, dot decimals, numeric elapsed timestamps. Column names can vary. Blank readings remain missing; unselected sensors are absent. Imports are limited to 1 MiB and 5,000 rows.

    Confirm the import

    Suggestions need your confirmation. Units describe the source file; conversions are explicit. Raw voltage needs a separate calibration and cannot be mapped directly to temperature or pH.

  2. 02 · Experiment and evidence

    Replay a fault to test detection. Whole-recording quality control is separate from the cursor preview.

    Compare with a reference or repeat run

    A changing signal alone is not evidence of drift. Declare your reference and tolerance. Patchline compares actual aligned readings without extrapolation.

    No comparison recording loaded.

    Offset: current time minus comparison time, in seconds. Match distance limits timestamp mismatch. Reference step limits flag abrupt reference changes for review. Neither establishes calibration accuracy.

    Independent recorded-data proof

    The bundled sample preserves S1_Temp from the first 5,000 records of Singh and Chaudhari’s Room Occupancy Estimation dataset, UCI, CC BY 4.0. Only timestamps were converted to elapsed seconds; temperature values and original dates/times are retained. This demonstrates environmental recording interoperability.
