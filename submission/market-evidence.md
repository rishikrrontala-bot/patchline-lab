# Patchline Lab — market evidence

**Verified:** 10 October 2026. **Validation status:** no buyer interviews, paid pilots, purchase commitments or partnerships recorded.

Patchline Lab's initial customer hypothesis is a small research team developing wound sensors: someone who needs to inspect experimental recordings, reproduce injected measurement failures and explain quality findings to colleagues. The examples below establish that this research activity exists. They do **not** establish a need for our tool, willingness to pay, compatibility, endorsement or a relationship with Patchline Lab.

**Inference to test:** teams combining sensor hardware and software may need a repeatable measurement-quality workflow. These teams also build their own software; their existing notebooks, scripts and vendor tools may already solve the problem. They are discovery examples, not three acquired customers or a market-size estimate. Research funding is not a budget available to Patchline Lab.

## Three interview questions

1. **Recent workflow:** “Walk me through the last sensor recording you checked. Which tools did you use, what went wrong, and where did you spend time?” Request only a non-patient, shareable benchtop example if they volunteer one.
2. **Useful proof:** “How do you currently test missing readings, spikes and timestamp gaps, and preserve the exact evidence in a report? What would an external tool have to do better?” Compare their current task with the same task in Patchline Lab; record observed time and errors rather than claiming savings in advance.
3. **Buying decision:** “Who approves a small software pilot, which budget could pay for it, and what integration or licensing constraints would rule it out?” Then test an explicitly proposed price and deliverable; distinguish a positive reaction from a concrete purchase commitment.
