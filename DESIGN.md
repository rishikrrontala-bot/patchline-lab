# Patchline Lab design world

An experimental recording on a researcher's workbench: calm, precise and readable. The main visual is the working measurement-to-evidence flow. This edition extends Patchline independently for Next Founders; treatment controls and clinical interpretations have no place in the interface.

## Visual system

Bone #F4F1EA, ink #151A18, gray-green #667670, terra #CE5B3A, rule #C9D0C9 and warning red #A74236. Restrained system serif display (Iowan/Baskerville), Helvetica-style body, system monospace measurement labels. Hairline divisions, clear numbered sections, large plots and intentional whitespace. No shadows, decorative clinical photography, remote fonts or generic card grid. Built by Rishik Rontala appears visibly and in author metadata.

## Composition and behavior

Four connected steps: map a recording and units, investigate exact evidence with reference or repeat comparison, run the separate synthetic benchmark, and export a reproducible workspace. The primary wow moment is a gradual discrepancy that passes shape-only checks but becomes visible against an explicit control. Optional channels are visibly absent, rather than invented. Protocol and finance settings live in clear labelled forms; applied settings remain distinct from edits. Provenance stays next to the recording. Whole-recording quality checks remain distinct from replay cursor values. Findings lead directly to their own source samples; color is supported by words and indices. Business assumptions are clearly labeled and separate from measured software results.

Keyboard focus, semantic controls, responsive tables and reduced motion are part of the system. Changes must preserve focus and stop replay when a recording changes. Print view reads as an experiment note with source, protocol, original/derived summary, evidence and limitations. Data never depend on animation. Mobile views retain the complete workflow.
